How to Play Arrow Escape

Master the simple yet deep mechanics of arrow unblocking logic.

1

Inspect the Line of Sight

Each arrow on the grid points in a specific direction (UP, DOWN, LEFT, or RIGHT). Before tapping an arrow, trace its projected exit line to the edge of the board.

2

Tap to Launch Free Arrows

If an arrow's forward path is 100% clear of all other arrows and obstacles, tap it! The arrow will launch off the board with a satisfying sound and visual effect.

3

Avoid Collisions & Manage Hearts

Tapping an arrow blocked by another arrow causes a collision mistake! The board will shake, a warning sound plays, and you lose 1 of your 3 level hearts. Losing all 3 hearts fails the level.

4

Unblock Chained Pathways

Clearing one free arrow opens up empty space on the grid, freeing previously blocked arrows. Plan your sequence carefully to untangle the whole board!

5

Use Hints & Undos Wisely

Stuck on a tricky board? Tap the 💡 Hint button to highlight the next valid arrow in the escape sequence, or tap ↩ Undo to step back your last move.
