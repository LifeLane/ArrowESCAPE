Arrow Escape Features

Built from the ground up for smooth performance, spatial logic depth, and clean design.

🧩

500+ Escalating Puzzles

Progression spans from beginner 4x4 boards up to expert 8x8 grids. Includes straight arrows, multi-turn bent arrows, fixed obstacles, and complex chained dependencies.

🧠

Deterministic Solver Engine

Powered by an internal Breadth-First Search (BFS) backtracking algorithm, ensuring zero unwinnable levels and accurate hint highlights.

💖

3-Hearts Health System

Every level grants 3 hearts. Tapping blocked arrows triggers mistake feedback and consumes 1 heart, rewarding strategic thinking over random tapping.

🔊

Procedural Audio Engine

Features real-time PCM sound generation for tactile click responses, ascending multi-note escape arpeggios, mistake thuds, and level fanfares.

💾

Offline-First Room Storage

All level progress, star ratings, best moves, daily streaks, and user settings persist locally on your device via SQLite Room database.

🎨

Dynamic Visual Themes

Customize your canvas with four distinct themes: Classic Light, Midnight Dark, Cyber Neon, and Warm Wood.
