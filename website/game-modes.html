🎯 GAME MODES

Explore Ways to Play

From progressive campaign levels to date-seeded daily puzzles and endless zen practice.

🏆

Level Mode

500+ hand-crafted and procedurally generated logic puzzles ranging from 4x4 beginner grids to 8x8 master levels. Guaranteed 100% solvable with 3-heart challenge rules.

📅

Daily Challenge

A fresh date-seeded (YYYYMMDD) puzzle every day at midnight! Maintain your daily streak, earn bonus stars, and compete on equal footing with players worldwide.

⚡

Challenge Mode

High-stakes puzzle solving with strict move limits and tighter collision penalties for experienced logic enthusiasts.

🧘

Zen Mode

Relaxing, pressure-free puzzle play. Unlimited hearts, zero move counters, and soothing procedural synthesizer audio for unhurried spatial reasoning.
