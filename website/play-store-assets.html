2. Full Description (Play Store Text)

Tap unobstructed arrows, unblock tangled pathways, and clear the board in Arrow Escape!

Arrow Escape is a crisp, satisfying, and challenging spatial logic puzzle game designed to sharpen your mind. Each arrow on the grid points in a specific direction. Trace forward along its line of sight—if its path to the edge of the board is completely unobstructed by other arrows, tap it to launch it off the board!

Every cleared arrow opens up empty space on the grid, freeing previously blocked arrows and triggering rewarding chain reactions. But think twice before you tap: launching a blocked arrow triggers a collision mistake and costs one of your 3 hearts!
