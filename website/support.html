Support & Knowledge Base

Find quick answers to common questions about Arrow Escape features, purchases, and troubleshooting.

How do I restore my Premium purchase on a new device?

Make sure you are logged into the same Google Account on Google Play that made the original purchase. Open Arrow Escape, navigate to Settings or the Premium Upgrade Screen, and tap "Restore Purchases". The app will securely query Google Play Billing and instantly unlock your permanent Ad-Free status.

Are all 500+ levels really solvable?

Yes, 100%! Every level layout generated by Arrow Escape undergoes a strict real-time Breadth-First Search (BFS) verification pass by our deterministic solver engine. There are zero unwinnable or broken levels.

How do hints work?

When you tap the 💡 Hint button during gameplay, our puzzle engine calculates the optimal unblocking path and highlights the exact arrow you should tap next with a cyan pulse effect. Standard free players start with 3 hints and can earn extra hints by watching rewarded ads or completing daily challenges. Premium players enjoy unlimited hints.

Can I play Arrow Escape without an internet connection?

Absolutely! Arrow Escape is engineered offline-first. Your entire level progress, stars, best move counts, and daily streaks are saved in your local Room database on device. An internet connection is only needed if you wish to view optional rewarded ads for extra hints.

Why is audio not playing?

First, check that your device is not set to Silent or Do Not Disturb mode. Then open in-game Settings and confirm that the Sound Effects toggle is enabled. Arrow Escape uses procedural audio synthesis that respects system volume levels.

How do I reset my game progress?

If you wish to start fresh from Level 1, go to Android device Settings → Apps → Arrow Escape → Storage and select "Clear Data", or use the Data Deletion request process on our website.
