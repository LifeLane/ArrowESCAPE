Data Deletion & Account Policy

Google Play Data Safety Compliance | Arrow Escape (`com.mitsara.arrowescape`)

Local Storage Clarification

Arrow Escape does NOT require or create cloud user accounts, email logins, or social profile registrations. All player data—including level completion scores, star counts, hint balances, and settings—is stored exclusively on your physical Android device inside your encrypted SQLite Room database.

Option A: Immediate On-Device Data Eradication

You can instantly delete 100% of your stored game data and preferences directly on your Android device at any time without submitting a support request:

  1. Open your Android device Settings app.
  2. Navigate to Apps (or App Manager) → Arrow Escape.
  3. Tap Storage & Cache.
  4. Tap "Clear Storage" or "Clear Data".

This action immediately and permanently removes all local database files, progress, and saved preferences.
