Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 27, 2026 | Application: Arrow Escape (`com.mitsara.arrowescape`)

1. Overview & Offline-First Principles

Mitsara Games ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Android application Arrow Escape (`com.mitsara.arrowescape`). Our application is designed with an offline-first architecture. Your game progress, unlocked level history, star ratings, daily streaks, and settings are stored locally on your device using SQLite Room database encryption. We do not collect, transmit, or sell personal identifiers such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers.

2. Advertising & Analytics Data

The standard free version of Arrow Escape uses Google AdMob to serve non-intrusive banner and interstitial advertisements. AdMob may collect anonymized diagnostic data, device advertising IDs (AAID), and coarse location data to deliver relevant ads and prevent fraud in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies.

If you upgrade to Arrow Escape Premium, all advertising SDKs are completely disabled, resulting in a 100% ad-free experience with zero ad tracking.

3. GDPR / CCPA Consent Controls

In compliance with European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), users are presented with a consent dialog upon initial launch. You can modify your ad personalization preferences at any time in the app settings menu under Settings → Personalized Ads (GDPR).

4. In-App Billing Transactions

In-app billing transactions for Premium status or hint packages are processed directly through Google Play Billing Services. Mitsara Games never accesses or stores your payment card details or banking credentials.

5. Data Deletion & Contact

Because your game data is stored locally on your Android device, you can completely erase all app data at any time by clearing application storage in Android Settings or using the in-app Data Reset option. For data privacy inquiries, visit our Delete Account / Data Request Page or contact support@mitsara.com.
