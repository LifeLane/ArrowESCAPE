Date-Seeded Generation

Every day at 00:00 local time, Arrow Escape generates a unique 6x6 or 7x7 board layout tailored specifically for that calendar date.

Daily Streak Tracking

Complete consecutive daily puzzles to build your active streak! Your streak counter is saved securely in your offline Room database.

Bonus Star Rewards

Solving daily puzzles awards 3 bonus stars towards your global player profile rating and unlocks hint refill packages.

How Daily Challenge Seeding Works

Daily boards use a deterministic hash based on year, month, and day (`YYYYMMDD`). This guarantees every player across the globe receives the exact same daily puzzle layout for friendly competition and shared puzzle discussion!
